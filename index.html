OUR STORY

Built from
silence.

Aurelia began with a question: what if a hotel could feel less like a hotel?

We found our answer in the landscape. Raw stone, open skies, traditional craft and spaces designed around natural light. The result is an estate that feels rooted rather than built.

Today, every detail is still guided by the same principle: hospitality should feel personal, never performative.
